Welcome to the operational master guide for running the New Matter MOD-t 3D printer offline using UltiMaker Cura and the MOD-t Printer Utility on macOS. Operating the MOD-t requires custom G-code routines, strict material selection (PLA ONLY), proper filament storage, precise movement speed throttling, and specific bed adhesion rules to account for its unique unheated flexible build plate and pinion-gear movement system.

⚠️

Legacy Offline Operations (Important Notice)

New Matter closed its operations in 2018. This MOD-t operates 100% locally and offline.

💾 Firmware Pre-Updated

The printer's firmware is already flashed to the latest version. Do not attempt to update the firmware. The original update servers are offline, and flashing will fail or brick the machine.

🔌 USB-Only Control

Since the original cloud backend is defunct, the printer's Wi-Fi network and setup button are non-functional. All printing and calibration must be done via local USB.

🛠️ 100% Offline Workflow

Use Cura 15.04.6 (legacy macOS) or modern command-line utilities (macOS Catalina+) to print. G-code files are loaded locally without needing external accounts.

Strict Material Rule: 1.75mm PLA Filament Only

NEVER use ABS, PETG, TPU/Flexible, or Wood/Metal filled filaments. The MOD-t features an unheated bed (PLA only adheres to unheated trays with rafts) and a light-duty extrusion gear that will jam or strip with abrasive/high-temp polymers.

🧵 Filament Storage & Moisture Management Guidelines

Quality Protection

PLA is hygroscopic—it rapidly absorbs ambient humidity from the air. Moist PLA creates steam pockets inside the 210°C hotend, leading to popping/crackling noises, extreme stringing, micro-clogs, and brittle filament snapping inside the extruder path.

📦 1. Airtight Dry Storage

Always store spools in airtight plastic bins or heavy-duty Ziploc vacuum bags with fresh desiccant silica packs when not actively printing.

🌡️ 2. Temperature & Light

Store in a cool, dry room (< 25°C / 77°F). Avoid direct sunlight, radiator heat, or damp basement environments which degrade PLA elasticity.

🥨 3. The "Bend Test" & Drying

Bend the end of the strand 90°. If it snaps cleanly like a dry pretzel, moisture is present. Dry the spool in a filament dryer at 45–50°C for 4 hours before use.

⚡ The 4 Mandatory Operating Rules

Non-Negotiable
1. Origin at Center (X0 Y0) Critical

The MOD-t homes coordinates to the center of the print tray, not the front-left corner. Slicing with standard bottom-left origin will cause immediate axis crashes.

2. Mandatory Raft Adhesion Adhesion

Raw PLA will not stick to the unheated plastic tray. A Raft with a 5.0mm extra margin provides a stable foundation and prevents thermal curling.

3. Z-Hop Enabled (2.0 mm) Hardware Safety

Lifts the nozzle during travel moves so it clears curling plastic edges without catching and dislodging the flexible magnetic build plate.

4. Cap Speed (≤40mm/s) & Accel (500mm/s²) Motion Guard

The under-bed pinion drive gear relies on friction tracks. High speeds or sudden acceleration cause gear teeth to skip, leading to severe diagonal layer shifting.

🔄 How to Swap Filament Using the MOD-t App

Desktop App Utility Workflow

Because the MOD-t lacks a screen or direct hardware unloading buttons on the chassis, all filament changes (loading new spools or removing spent filament) must be commanded via USB using the MOD-t Printer Tool app (or CLI utility).

📤 Phase 1: Unload Current Filament Step-by-Step
  1. 1
    Connect USB & Power On: Connect Mac to MOD-t via USB cable and launch MOD-t Printer Tool.
  2. 2
    Select "Unload Filament": Click Filament > Unload Filament in the top menu or dashboard.
  3. 3
    Wait for Preheat (~210 °C): The app will signal the hotend to heat up. The front MOD-t button LED will pulse.
  4. 4
    Automatic Motor Reverse: Once heated, the drive gears run in reverse to retract filament up the tube. Pull strand out gently once free.
📥 Phase 2: Load New PLA Filament Step-by-Step
  1. 1
    Snip Filament Tip at 45°: Cut the end of the new PLA strand at a sharp 45-degree angle. Never feed flat or mushroomed ends.
  2. 2
    Select "Load Filament": Click Filament > Load Filament in the MOD-t Printer Tool app.
  3. 3
    Insert Strand into Carriage: Feed strand into the top entrance hole until it touches drive gears. Hold light downward pressure.
  4. 4
    Purge & Complete: The drive gears pull the strand in and extrude plastic out the nozzle. Once pure color flows, click Done / Stop.
💡 Terminal CLI Alternative (macOS 10.15+): Open Terminal and execute: modt-tool load or modt-tool unload

📥 Option A: Official MOD-t Desktop App v1.5.3 DMG ↗

  3. Commands: modt-tool load, modt-tool unload, modt-tool print model.gcode.
🎯 Mandatory USB Bed Calibration Procedure:

Before running your first print shift or after moving the printer, perform a 3-point tray calibration using the MOD-t Printer Tool:

  1. Connect Mac to printer via USB cable and launch MOD-t Printer Tool.
  2. Select Settings > Calibrate Build Tray.
  3. Follow wizard prompts: nozzle touches 3 corner points on the plastic tray to store Z-offset.
  4. Verify calibration paper slip slides with light friction under nozzle tip.

Hardware & Quality Troubleshooting Matrix

Select a symptom below to view root causes, physical fixes, and step-by-step procedures for clearing stuck filament jams.

⚠️ Symptom 1: Layer Shifting / Diagonal Skewing Bed Gear Skipping

Drive pinions skipping teeth on bottom tray gear tracks due to motion resistance or excessive acceleration.

Recommended Fixes:
  • Verify M204 S500 is present in Start G-code.
  • Reduce print speed to 30–40 mm/s max.
  • Clean debris out of under-bed gear teeth and wipe metal guide rods clean with Isopropyl Alcohol.
💥 Symptom 2: Nozzle Collides with Print / Tray Dislodged Travel Collision

Plastic edges curling upward on unheated bed during travel moves and catching hotend tip.

Recommended Fixes:
  • Verify Z Hop When Retracting is set to 2.0 mm.
  • Ensure print fan is running at 100% speed after layer 2.
💨 Symptom 3: First Layer Warping / Peeling Adhesion Loss

Lack of thermal adhesion on unheated tray surface causing PLA raft corners to curl up.

Recommended Fixes:
  • Ensure Raft build plate adhesion is enabled with 5.0mm extra margin.
  • Thoroughly degrease clear build plate with 90%+ Isopropyl Alcohol before every print.
⚙️ Symptom 4: Extruder Clicking / No Plastic Flowing Heat Creep / Wet Filament

PLA softening prematurely in hotend throat (Heat Creep) or wet filament popping in the nozzle.

Recommended Fixes:
  • Ensure spool is 1.75mm PLA (no ABS/PETG) and has been stored in a dry box.
  • Keep printing temperature strictly at 210 °C to reduce melt chamber pressure.
  • Check filament spool for unraveling drag or tension.
  • Perform Routine A (Cold Pull) or Routine B below.

Hotend Melt Throat Dynamics

Understanding why 210 °C is the sweet spot for the long MOD-t hotend throat to balance viscosity against heat creep risk.

Jammed Filament Clearing Routines

Routine A: The Cold Pull (Filament Intact Above Carriage) Soft Pull
  1. Heat hotend to 220 °C via control or G-code.
  2. Depress lever & push filament by hand until plastic oozes.
  3. Turn off heater and cool down to exactly 90 °C.
  4. Press lever & pull filament firmly upward with pliers until plug pops free.
Routine B: Snapped Off Flush Inside Hotend 1.5mm Wrench Push
  1. Heat hotend to 210 °C.
  2. Press top extruder tension lever down.
  3. Insert a 1.5 mm Allen wrench into top entry hole.
  4. Push downward firmly to force stuck plastic through heated nozzle tip.
Routine C: Stripped / Ground Filament Manual Bypass
  1. Heat hotend to 210 °C.
  2. Fully depress spring-loaded lever to disengage drive gears.
  3. Manually push fresh filament into top port to force ground segment out.
